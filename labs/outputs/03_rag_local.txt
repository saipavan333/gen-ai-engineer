4) Dense vs BM25 vs hybrid (RRF), scored against the doc a human says is right
======================================================================
  'what does RF-BANK mean'                      gold=ref-02   dense=#1  bm25=#1  hybrid=#1
  'warranty on MX-750'                          gold=sell-01  dense=#1  bm25=#1  hybrid=#1
  'can I pay in instalments'                    gold=pay-02   dense=#2  bm25=miss  hybrid=miss
  'my parcel came broken, what do I do'         gold=ret-02   dense=#1  bm25=#1  hybrid=#1
  'is there a fee if my cart is small'          gold=ship-01  dense=#1  bm25=#1  hybrid=#1
  'someone on the phone asked for my OTP'       gold=acc-01   dense=#1  bm25=#1  hybrid=#1
  hit@3 over 6 queries: dense=6/6, bm25=5/6, hybrid=5/6
  Look: BM25 misses the paraphrase ('instalments' never appears; the doc says 'EMI'). Fusion is not free:
  RRF gives a noisy BM25 list the same vote as a good dense list, so it can push a right answer
  out of the top 3. On this tiny corpus dense already wins; hybrid earns its keep on big corpora full of
  near-identical codes (SKUs, error ids) that embeddings blur. Never assume: measure hit@k (lab 06).
