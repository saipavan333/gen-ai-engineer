  hit@3 over n=20 queries (95% Wilson intervals):
    dense   20/20 = 100% (95% CI 84%-100%)
    bm25    18/20 = 90% (95% CI 70%-97%)
    hybrid  18/20 = 90% (95% CI 70%-97%)
  With n=20 the intervals overlap heavily: a difference of one or two queries is NOT evidence that one
  method is better. You need hundreds of labelled queries for that (lab 06 shows how the interval shrinks).
  Look: BM25 misses the paraphrase ('instalments' never appears; the doc says 'EMI'). Fusion is not free:
  RRF gives a noisy BM25 list the same vote as a good dense list, so it can push a right answer
  out of the top 3. On this tiny corpus dense already wins; hybrid earns its keep on big corpora full of
  near-identical codes (SKUs, error ids) that embeddings blur. Never assume: measure hit@k (lab 06).
