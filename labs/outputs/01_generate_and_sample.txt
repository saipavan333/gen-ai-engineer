======================================================================
  seed=1: Bengaluru in July typically experiences hot and dry weather with temperatures often exceeding 30°C.
  seed=2: Bengaluru in July typically experiences hot and dry weather with temperatures often exceeding 30°C.
  seed=3: Bengaluru in July typically experiences hot and dry weather with temperatures often exceeding 30°C.
  distinct outputs at T=0: 1
  Look: T=0 is close to greedy but NOT a determinism guarantee. In one of our runs only 2 of 3 seeds
  matched, in another all 3 did: GPU kernels, batching and tie-breaking can still change a token.
  Never build a test on exact text.
  Also check the FACT: Bengaluru in July is monsoon season (mild and wet). A fluent, confident
  'hot and dry' answer is a hallucination - fluency is not accuracy (Lesson 1.6).
